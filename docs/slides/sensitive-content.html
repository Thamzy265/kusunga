Sensitive-content protection

Keep copies from password managers out of history, and pause history for a while from the menu bar or tray.

Use the arrow keys to move between slides · Print to save as PDF

Problem and users

Some things you copy should never land in history

Who

Anyone who copies passwords, one-time codes, bank details or private messages.

The job they are doing

Copying a password from a password manager, or pasting bank details into a form, without leaving a copy behind.

Today's pain

Kusunga already skips copies that apps mark as private (M1.2). A copy with no mark is kept, and there is no way to stop recording for a while.

Success measure

Copies from listed apps, or made while paused, are never read. Pausing takes 2 clicks. You can always see that history is paused, and until when.

Plan item M1.6 (docs/PLAN.md). Details: docs/design/sensitive-content.md, docs/ui/sensitive-content.md, ADR 0005.

Scope for this phase

One app list for history and shortcuts, and a pause you can always see

In this phase

  • Copies from apps on the ignore list are not kept and not read
  • The list starts with common password managers. You can add and remove apps, as in M1.5
  • Pause history for 15 minutes, 1 hour or until you resume, from the menu bar / tray and from the app
  • A banner in History and the tray tooltip say history is paused, and until when. Resume is one click
  • A new Privacy tab holds the pause and the list

Deferred (Later lines with triggers)

  • Banking app presets, trigger: ≥ 3 beta requests naming a banking app
  • Ignoring a website or browser tab, trigger: ≥ 10 requests
  • Spotting card numbers or passwords by their content, trigger: ≥ 5 reports of a secret kept
  • A "copied from" label on history items, trigger: ≥ 10 requests

Options

Where Kusunga decides to skip a copy

Option A: in the small native part, before reading

  • The Mac and Windows code check pause and the app list before reading the copy, as they already do for private marks
  • A skipped copy is never read, and which app you copied from never reaches the main app
  • The pause ends on time even after the computer slept
  • Written twice (Mac and Windows). About 1.3× the effort

Option B: in the shared app code

  • One copy of the rule
  • Every copy is read and sent to the main app with its app name, then dropped if it should be skipped
  • The pause relies on a timer that can run late after sleep

Recommendation

Option A, so the promise "copies from these apps aren't kept" also means they are never read (ADR 0005).

Security

Any app can write to the clipboard, so the checks only ever make Kusunga keep less

Trust boundaries

  • The clipboard and the "source app" information, which any app can set
  • The tray menu, which anyone at the computer can use

Abuse cases

  • An app pretends to be a password manager so its copies aren't kept: this only hides its own copies
  • Someone at your unlocked computer pauses history: nothing more is kept, and the banner shows it
  • A copy from a password manager is credited to another app

Mitigations

  • Private marks are still checked first, and most password managers set them
  • On Mac, the app's own source label is used when present
  • On Windows, the clipboard owner is used, which is exact

Known limit on Mac: Kusunga checks the clipboard every 0.25 s, so a copy followed by a very fast switch to another app can be credited to the wrong app.

Plan and how you'll check it

Built in four steps, Mac first

1 Pause setting and new presets→ 2 Skip checks on Mac, then Windows→ 3 Tray menu and banner→ 4 Privacy tab

How we will check it

  • Automated: tests for each skip rule and the pause clock (including after sleep), and a check that no app name or text is logged or sent to the main app
  • QA on your Mac in the test profile: copies from an ignored app and while paused aren't kept; pause and resume from the menu bar; banner and tooltip. Screenshots and videos
  • What only you can check: VoiceOver on the new tab and menu

Decisions needed

5 decisions for you

1. One app list or two?

Options: A, one list for history and typed shortcuts; or B, two lists

Recommendation: A, because an app you don't want recorded is one where you don't want shortcuts either

2. Banking apps in the preset list?

Options: A, password managers only, with a Later line for banking apps; or B, add banking apps now

Recommendation: A. Few UK and EU banks have desktop apps, and their IDs can't be checked here. The plan's wording changes to "password managers"

3. Does a pause survive a restart?

Options: A, yes; or B, a restart resumes history

Recommendation: A, so a restart never quietly undoes a privacy choice

4. Where in the app?

Options: A, a new Privacy tab for pause and the list; or B, the list stays in Snippets, and pause is in the tray and a banner only

Recommendation: A, because the list now covers history too

5. On Mac, which app counts as the source?

Options: A, the app's own source label when present, otherwise the front app; or B, always the front app

Recommendation: A, because it's right even for copies made in the background
